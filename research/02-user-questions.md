## Key Takeaways for Product Architecture
1. **The "Household Measure" Gap:** Questions 19–38 clearly demonstrate that Indian users do not weigh grams; they eat in katoris, rotis, pieces, and spoons. A system that forces 100g inputs will fail.
2. **The "Vegetarian Protein Puzzle":** Questions 39–56 reveal persistent anxiety regarding protein on vegetarian diets. A built-in "Protein per 100 Calorie" index for Indian ingredients is an essential feature.
3. **The "Cooking Variable":** Desi ghee, tadka oil, and sugar in tea are the three biggest invisible calorie leaks in Indian households. The tool must surface these variables transparently.
